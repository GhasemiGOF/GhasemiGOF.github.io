This is a template for my January research log. The theme for this version is **beginning-of-year calibration** — not resolutions, but an honest inventory of what I believe, what I have evidence for, and what I am guessing.

<span class="margin-note">January is for naming assumptions before the year accelerates. A log that only lists goals is a wish list, not a notebook.</span>

A research month rarely resolves into a clean story while it is happening. If I wait until everything is clear, I lose the details that would have helped me understand how the work actually developed.
